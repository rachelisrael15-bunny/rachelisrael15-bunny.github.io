Treble Note Invaders

Goal: Read the note on the treble staff on the right, then send a quarter note from your mini piano keyboard to meet the falling letter that matches that note name.

How to play:

  • Look at the target note on the treble staff.
  • Move your mini piano with ← and →.
  • Press Space to send a quarter note upward.
  • Hit the correct matching letter to score points.

You will play 3 rounds of 60 seconds each. Try to get your highest score!
